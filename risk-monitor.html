Expert Advisor · read-only

Every open trade, and exactly what happens if it hits target — or stop.

Risk Monitor sits on one chart and lists every position open on your account, whatever symbol it's on. Risk and reward are measured from each trade's entry price, so the numbers stay fixed — they only move if you actually change the stop-loss or take-profit.

What it does

Account-wide view

Lists every open position across all symbols, whether it was opened manually or by another EA — not just what's on the current chart.

Committed risk and reward

Risk $ and Reward $ are calculated from your entry price to the stop-loss and take-profit. They stay static as price moves, and only change if you move the SL or TP yourself.

Missing-stop warning

Any trade without a stop-loss or take-profit is flagged clearly ("No SL" / "No TP") instead of silently showing a zero.

Portfolio totals

A totals row sums risk, reward, and floating profit/loss across everything open, for a quick read on total account exposure.

Read-only

This EA never sends, modifies, or closes an order — it only reads position data and draws a panel. Safe to run alongside any other EA or manual trading.

Install

  1. Download OpenTrades_RiskMonitor.mq5 below.
  2. In MT5, open MetaEditor (press F4), then use File → Open and browse to your MQL5/Experts folder — or find it faster via MT5's File → Open Data Folder.
  3. Place the downloaded file in that Experts folder, then open it in MetaEditor and compile with F7.
  4. Back in MT5, refresh the Navigator panel (right-click → Refresh) and find it under Expert Advisors.
  5. Drag it onto any one chart. No inputs to configure — it starts listing your open trades immediately.

Worth knowing: the money conversion uses each symbol's own broker-reported tick value, accurate for the vast majority of FX pairs. A handful of exotic instruments (some stock or futures CFDs with non-linear contract specs) can throw that off slightly — worth a quick sanity check against your broker's own figures.
